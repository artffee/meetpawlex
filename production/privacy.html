Privacy Policy

This policy describes how meetpawlex (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects information when you use meetpawlex.com or the meetpawlex software product. We aim for plain English — if a section confuses you, write to hello@meetpawlex.com and we'll clarify.

1. Information We Collect

  • Waitlist data: first name, last name, business email, business name (optional), business type, estimated monthly customers (optional), your free-text response about your biggest challenge (optional), and a timestamped record of consent to application-related contact. Please do not include client, pet medical, or other sensitive records in the form.
  • Product usage: when you run meetpawlex, the commands you invoke and basic timing/cost metrics, used to improve routing and reliability. Document contents you analyze stay on your machine and your Anthropic account — we do not collect them.
  • Waitlist security: the server uses your network address to check requests and derives a daily keyed identifier for rate limiting. The waitlist application record does not contain your IP address. Rate-limit records are removed after at least 48 hours by a daily cleanup job. Vercel may separately process request metadata to host and secure the service.

2. How We Use It

  • To manage your beta application and contact you about access. The waitlist form does not opt you into marketing.
  • To prioritize features against the real challenges users describe.
  • To diagnose problems and improve cost routing.

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share contract contents, client lists, or pet records with third parties.
  • We do not run third-party advertising trackers on this site.

4. Subprocessors

We rely on a small number of vendors to operate the service:

  • Anthropic — the model provider behind every meetpawlex command. Your API key is yours; their terms apply to your usage.
  • Vercel — hosts this website and processes waitlist applications in server-side functions. Applications are stored in a private Vercel Blob store in the US East region and are accessible only to authorized account administrators.
  • Resend (if enabled) — sends application alerts to PawLex administrators. These alerts do not include applicant names, email addresses, or application details. Email alerts are currently disabled pending provider setup.

5. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it entirely. Email hello@meetpawlex.com from the address you signed up with. You may also withdraw your application or ask us to stop application-related emails. We retain applications while managing beta access unless you request deletion; we review retention when the beta closes.

6. Security

We use industry-standard transport encryption (TLS) and access controls. Applicant details are not written to browser logs or public files. We will handle any security incident and required notices under applicable law.

7. Children

meetpawlex is a B2B product. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

8. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change, including the date above.
