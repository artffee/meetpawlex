02 / The Problem

The pet industry runs on handshakes and hope.

$150B in annual spending. 11% growth through every recession. And the people running it are buried in paperwork, flying blind on compliance, and operating without the legal protection they need.

Compliance Blind Spots

Licensing rules differ by state, county, and service type. Most pet businesses don't know what they don't know — until a fine or shutdown notice arrives.

No Legal Armor

74% of groomers and trainers operate without proper contracts. One incident with a client's pet can wipe out years of work overnight.

Zero Business Visibility

Spreadsheets and gut feelings. No clarity on revenue trends, client retention, or where the business is leaking money every month.

Admin Steals the Day

Pet professionals spend 30%+ of their time on paperwork — time stolen from the animals and clients they built the business to serve.

Insurance Gaps

The #1 missed coverage in pet businesses is animal bailee — quietly excluded by most standard GL policies. Owners discover this at claim time.

Lawyers Cost $300/hr

Local attorneys aren't scalable for a salon billing $80 a groom. The economics don't work — so most operators just go without.

“One missed license renewal. One client incident without a proper waiver. One bad contract clause. Any of these can end a business overnight.”

05 / The 5-Agent Review System

Five specialists, working in parallel.

The flagship /pawlex review command launches five specialized AI agents simultaneously, each analyzing the contract from a different angle. The results synthesize into a single Contract Safety Score (0–100) with a letter grade.
